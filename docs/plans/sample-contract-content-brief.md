# Software Subscription Agreement

This Software Subscription Agreement is between Sample Software Co., a Delaware corporation (“Provider”), and Example Customer Co., a California corporation (“Customer”). It takes effect on January 1, 2027. The Order Form and this agreement together form the “Agreement.”

## 1. Subscription and use

Provider will make Workspace Cloud, its hosted project and document collaboration software, available to Customer during the subscription term. Customer may authorize the number of users stated in the Order Form to access the service for Customer’s internal business purposes. Customer may replace users without increasing that number. Additional users require a written order agreed by both parties.

Customer is responsible for its users, account credentials, and the information it uploads (“Customer Data”). Customer must have the rights needed to provide Customer Data and must use the service lawfully. Customer must not resell access, circumvent access controls, introduce malicious code, or use the service to infringe others’ rights. Provider may process Customer Data only to deliver and secure the service, provide support, and meet its legal obligations. Provider will not sell Customer Data or use it to train general-purpose AI models.

Provider owns the service and its documentation. Customer owns Customer Data. Each party retains its other intellectual property; no ownership transfers under this Agreement.

## 2. Support and service warranty

Provider will provide email support during the hours in the Order Form and will use reasonable efforts to respond within one business day. A business day is Monday through Friday, excluding California state holidays. The response target is not a resolution guarantee. Provider may perform maintenance and will give advance notice of planned material interruptions when reasonably practicable.

Provider warrants that the service will materially conform to its user documentation. Provider must not change that documentation to remove a material function during the current subscription term. Customer must describe a material failure in writing. If Provider cannot correct it within 30 days after receiving notice, Customer may terminate the affected subscription by written notice and receive a prorated refund of prepaid fees for the unused portion of the term, with no further fees due for that portion. This is Customer’s exclusive remedy for a breach of this service warranty. It does not limit rights or remedies for a separate breach of another provision.

Except for this express warranty, neither party makes implied warranties to the extent permitted by law. Provider does not promise uninterrupted or error-free operation.

## 3. Fees and payment

The annual subscription fee, currency, invoice schedule, and payment period are stated in the Order Form. Provider will invoice at the beginning of each subscription year. Customer must pay each invoice within 30 days after receiving it. Fees exclude applicable sales taxes, which Customer must pay; Provider remains responsible for taxes on its own income.

Customer must notify Provider of an invoice dispute within 30 days after receiving the invoice, describe the disputed amount and reason, and pay the undisputed portion on time. The parties will work promptly and in good faith to resolve the dispute. This notice period does not prevent correction of a duplicate charge or clear billing error. No late charges accrue on a reasonably disputed amount while the parties resolve it; any amount determined to be due is payable within 15 days after resolution.

On overdue, undisputed amounts, Provider may charge simple interest at the lower of 1% per month (12% per year) or the maximum lawful rate. Provider may suspend access only if an undisputed payment remains overdue for at least 15 days after written notice identifying the amount and warning of suspension. Provider will restore access promptly after payment. Suspension does not extend the subscription term or remove Customer’s data-export rights under Section 7.

## 4. Term and renewal

The initial subscription term is 12 months, as dated in the Order Form. The subscription automatically renews for successive 12-month terms unless either party gives written nonrenewal notice at least 30 days before the current term ends.

Provider may increase the annual subscription fee only at renewal, by no more than 5% of the fee for the preceding subscription year, and only by giving written notice at least 60 days before the current term ends. Without timely notice, the existing fee applies for the next renewal term. Other commercial changes require both parties’ written agreement.

Neither party may terminate the subscription early for convenience. Nonrenewal ends the subscription at the end of the current term and does not create an additional charge.

## 5. Termination for cause

Either party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other materially breaches it and fails to cure the breach within 30 days after receiving written notice describing it. Either party may also terminate if the other ceases business operations or becomes subject to an insolvency proceeding that is not dismissed within 60 days, to the extent permitted by law.

If Customer terminates for Provider’s uncured material breach or insolvency, Provider will refund prepaid subscription fees for the unused portion of the term within 30 days, and Customer owes no subscription fees for that unused portion. If Provider terminates for Customer’s uncured material breach or insolvency, Customer remains responsible for the committed fees for the current subscription term according to the agreed invoice schedule; prepaid fees are not refundable. Neither outcome creates a commitment for a future renewal term.

When the Agreement ends, ordinary service access ends, subject to the export period in Section 7. Accrued payment obligations, confidentiality, ownership, data handling, and liability provisions survive as needed to give them effect. Section 9 continues to govern claims arising from use during the subscription term.

## 6. Confidentiality

“Confidential Information” means nonpublic information disclosed by one party to the other that is marked confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential. Customer Data is Customer’s Confidential Information. Information is excluded if the receiving party can show it was already lawfully known, becomes public without a breach, is independently developed, or is lawfully received from another source without a confidentiality duty.

The receiving party will use Confidential Information only to perform or exercise rights under this Agreement, protect it with reasonable care, and disclose it only to personnel and contractors who need it and are bound by confidentiality obligations. The receiving party is responsible for their compliance. A legally required disclosure is permitted, with advance notice when lawful and reasonable assistance at the disclosing party’s expense to limit disclosure.

These duties last for three years after the Agreement ends, and for trade secrets while they remain trade secrets. Customer Data remains protected for as long as the receiving party retains it.

## 7. Data and security

Provider will maintain reasonable technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to Customer Data, including encrypted transmission, access controls, and backups. Provider must ensure that service subcontractors handling Customer Data are bound by written confidentiality and security duties, and remains responsible for their performance under this Agreement.

Provider will notify Customer without undue delay, and no later than 72 hours after confirming unauthorized access to or disclosure of Customer Data in Provider’s or its service subcontractors’ systems. Provider will share available information about the incident, take reasonable containment and remediation measures, and provide material updates. Notice need not await completion of the investigation. Customer remains responsible for securing its own devices and credentials; Provider’s duties apply to any resulting incident in the systems described above.

Customer may export its project records and documents through the service during the term. For 30 days after expiration or termination, Provider will offer read-only access or a supported export of those records and documents in CSV and their original uploaded file formats, without an additional export fee. This access does not permit new projects or other ordinary use.

Provider will delete Customer Data from active systems within 60 days after the Agreement ends and from backups within 90 days, except where law requires retention. Retained data remains protected, is accessible only for the retention purpose, and will be deleted when that requirement ends.

Customer may upload ordinary business contact details but must not upload health records, payment-card information, or other sensitive personal information requiring additional contractual safeguards. If the intended use requires a data processing agreement or additional safeguards under applicable law, the parties must agree them in writing before that processing begins. This Agreement does not claim a security certification or supply an unlisted regulatory commitment.

## 8. Limits on liability

Neither party is liable under this Agreement for indirect, special, incidental, consequential, or punitive damages, or lost profits, revenue, or business opportunities, even if advised that they were possible. This exclusion does not exclude amounts payable to a third party under the express indemnity in Section 9; those amounts remain subject to the monetary cap below.

Each party’s total aggregate liability arising out of or relating to this Agreement, across all claims, is limited to **six months of subscription fees**. For this calculation, one month equals one-twelfth of the annual subscription fee payable for the subscription year in which the earliest event giving rise to any claim against that party occurred. The cap therefore equals half of that annual fee, regardless of invoice timing or fees paid so far. It excludes taxes and is not reduced by service refunds or credits. For an event after the subscription ends, use the final subscription year’s annual fee. At the initial $24,000 annual fee, the cap is $12,000, including on the first day of the term.

The exclusions and cap apply to contract, tort, and other claims, including confidentiality, security, and Section 9 indemnity claims. They do not limit Customer’s obligation to pay subscription fees and taxes properly due under the Agreement, Provider’s express obligation to return prepaid fees under Sections 2, 5, 9, or 12, either party’s fraud or willful misconduct, or liability that applicable law does not allow to be limited. Payment and refund exceptions cover those specified amounts only; associated damages remain subject to this section. Fraud and willful misconduct are express uncapped exceptions.

## 9. Third-party intellectual property claims

Provider will defend Customer against a third-party claim that the service, as supplied by Provider and used as permitted by this Agreement, infringes that third party’s copyright or patent, and will pay damages and costs finally awarded against Customer or included in a settlement approved by Provider, subject to Section 8.

Customer must promptly notify Provider of the claim and provide reasonable cooperation at Provider’s expense. Provider controls the defense and settlement, but may not admit fault on Customer’s behalf or impose a nonmonetary obligation on Customer without Customer’s written consent. A notice delay excuses Provider only to the extent the delay materially prejudices the defense.

This obligation excludes claims caused by Customer Data, modifications not made or authorized by Provider, combinations with products not supplied or required by Provider where the claim would not otherwise arise, or continued challenged use after Provider supplies a materially equivalent noninfringing alternative and reasonable migration time.

If a covered claim is made or reasonably likely, Provider may obtain continued use rights or modify or replace the affected service without materially reducing its functionality. If neither is commercially reasonable, either party may terminate the affected subscription; Provider will refund prepaid fees for the unused portion and Customer owes no further fees for that portion. This section is Customer’s exclusive remedy for third-party intellectual property infringement claims.

## 10. Publicity

Customer permits Provider to display Customer’s name and logo in a customer list on Provider’s website, following any brand guidelines Customer supplies. This permission does not allow testimonials, case studies, press releases, or an implied endorsement. Customer may withdraw permission by written notice; Provider must remove the name and logo within 10 business days. Permission ends when the Agreement ends, with removal within the same period.

## 11. Assignment

Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other’s written consent, which must not be unreasonably withheld, conditioned, or delayed. Either party may assign it without consent as part of a merger or a sale of substantially all of the assets of the business using or providing the service, if the successor assumes all obligations under this Agreement in writing. The assigning party must give prompt written notice. Any other attempted assignment without required consent is void.

## 12. Administration

California law and applicable federal laws of the United States govern this Agreement, without regard to conflict-of-laws rules. The parties submit to the state and federal courts located in San Francisco County, California. Each party bears its own legal fees unless a court awards costs under applicable law.

Contractual notices must be emailed to the notice addresses in the Order Form. A notice is received on the next business day after sending unless the sender receives a delivery-failure message. Either party may update its notice address by notice to the other.

This Agreement is the entire agreement on the subscription. The Order Form controls only the product, authorized-user count, subscription dates, annual fee, currency, invoice schedule, payment period, and contact details. Any other override must expressly identify the section changed and state the replacement term in a writing signed by both parties. Purchase-order boilerplate has no effect. Changes otherwise require both parties’ written agreement.

Neither party is responsible for delay caused by events beyond its reasonable control, except payment obligations, provided it promptly notifies the other and reasonably works to reduce the effect. If an event prevents material service delivery for more than 30 consecutive days, Customer may terminate and receive a prorated refund of prepaid fees for the unused portion, with no fees due for that portion.

Neither party’s failure to enforce a term waives it. If a provision is unenforceable, the remaining provisions continue in effect. The parties are independent contractors. Electronic signatures and counterparts are permitted.
